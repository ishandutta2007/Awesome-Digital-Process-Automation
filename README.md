# Awesome-Digital-Process-Automation

# Top Digital Process Automation Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on BPM, Low-Code Workflow, Case Management, Intelligent Automation & End-to-End Process Orchestration*  
**Last updated: October 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Digital Process Automation (DPA)**. These systems model, execute, and optimize business processes—from simple approvals to complex case management—across people, systems, and AI agents.

**Examples** include Appian, Creatio, Nintex, Pipefy, Kissflow, ProcessMaker, Bonitasoft, Bizagi, Pega, and ServiceNow (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: Process automation has an excellent open BPM stack. **Camunda**, **Flowable**, **Bonita**, **ProcessMaker**, and **jBPM** are production-proven. This section is heavily expanded.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Camunda](https://github.com/camunda/camunda-bpm-platform)**  
  Leading open-source BPMN workflow and decision engine—developer-centric process automation (Camunda 7 community; Camunda 8 and forks available).

- **[Flowable](https://github.com/flowable/flowable-engine)**  
  Open BPMN, CMMN, and DMN engine—embeddable process automation with a strong community and enterprise edition.

- **[Bonita](https://github.com/bonitasoft/bonita-engine)**  
  Full open-source BPM platform with low-code designers, forms, and process applications.

- **[ProcessMaker](https://github.com/ProcessMaker/processmaker)**  
  Open-source workflow and BPM platform with low-code design and operational process management.

- **[jBPM](https://github.com/kiegroup/jbpm)**  
  Open business process engine from the KIE/Drools ecosystem—BPMN and advanced process features.

- **[Activiti](https://github.com/Activiti/Activiti)**  
  Open lightweight BPMN engine (historical and continued community use).

- **[n8n, Activepieces, Windmill](https://github.com/n8n-io/n8n)**  
  Open workflow automation tools bridging integration (iPaaS-style) and lighter process automation.

- **[CIB seven & Camunda 7 continuity forks](https://cibseven.org/)**  
  Open-source continuity options for organizations staying on Camunda 7-style architectures.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **BPMN core**: Camunda or Flowable for developer-driven automation.
- **Low-code BPM**: Bonita or ProcessMaker for business-user design.
- **Rules + processes**: jBPM with Drools.
- **Composable stacks**: BPM engine + forms UI + identity (Keycloak) + document store.
- Commercial DPA still leads in packaged industry apps and enterprise support SLAs.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  
**Camunda**, **Flowable**, **Bonita**, or **ProcessMaker** as the process core.  
**n8n**-class tools for integration-heavy flows.  
Commercial platforms (Appian, Pega, ServiceNow, Creatio, Nintex, etc.) provide broader low-code and case suites.  
Many enterprises run open BPM engines successfully; regulated or citizen-developer programs often prefer commercial DPA. Fully open process automation is production-ready at scale.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- Automated processes can move money, data, and access. Design with least privilege, audit logs, and human checkpoints where required. Test changes carefully in regulated environments.
- Open-source engines offer control and standards (BPMN/DMN) but require development and operations capacity. Commercial platforms shift product and support burden to the vendor. Neither replaces clear process ownership and change management.
